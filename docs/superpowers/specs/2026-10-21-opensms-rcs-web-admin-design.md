Date: 2026-10-21
Branch: `feature/remote-connector-v1`

The product scope has since been expanded and approved as a multi-tenant SaaS platform. The canonical architecture is now:

- `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-opensms-rcs-saas-platform-design.md`
